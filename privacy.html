Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Who we are

Table and the City is an independent restaurant guide run by Egbert in Belgium. He is responsible for this website and the Table and the City app under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact: info@tableandthecity.com

The short version

There are no accounts or logins, no advertising, no analytics trackers and no tracking cookies. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

Forms

When you send a form (suggest a place, report a correction, request a city or an event), the text you type and, if you give it, your email address are sent to our mailbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). If FormSubmit is unavailable, the same message goes through Web3Forms (web3forms.com). We use it only to answer you and to improve the guide, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Maps, location and directions

Maps use tiles from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org): your browser loads them directly from OpenStreetMap’s servers, which see your IP address like any web server.

“Near me” asks for your location only when you tap it and you allow it. The position is used on your device to sort places by distance and is never sent to us.

The Directions button opens Google Maps; from there, Google’s own privacy policy applies.

Visit counter

We keep an anonymous visit counter run by Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev); it is not shown on the site. At most once a day your browser sends an anonymous request that adds one to the total. No cookie or identifier is used; like any web server, Abacus sees your IP address.

Other content from third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts and the map library from unpkg.com. Some restaurant photos load from Google (Places photos), and some place pages embed Instagram posts or profiles; Instagram (Meta) may set its own cookies inside that embed. These providers receive your IP address when your browser loads their content.

Stored on your device

A few small settings are kept in your browser’s local storage: your language, the last city you viewed, the day of your last counted visit, whether you dismissed a hint, and a limit on how often a form can be sent. The site and app also keep an offline copy of pages you have opened. None of this leaves your device, and you can clear it in your browser or app settings.

My table (your Want to go and Been here places, with your own scores and visit dates) and event reminders are also kept only on your device: they are never sent to us or anyone else, and they work offline. Reminders in the app are notifications scheduled by your phone itself (on the website, a calendar file you download); no push service is used.

With “Want to go with…” you can send a friend a link to a place. The first name you type is only put in that link and stored on your device; it is never sent to us.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to see, correct or delete any message you sent us, or object to how we use it. Just email us. You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Changes

We update this page whenever the site or the app changes what it does with data.
