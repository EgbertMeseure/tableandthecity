Terms in short

  • What you getExactly what the package says. Promoted places are always marked Promoted on the guide, and Instagram content carries Instagram’s paid partnership label.
  • When it runsGuide packages and the bundle are monthly subscriptions: they start in the month you pick and renew every month until you cancel; you pay the first month at checkout. The calendar shows which months and spots are still free. Instagram content goes up in the month you pick, on a date we agree with you.
  • Only places that fitWe only promote good independent places: no chains, no fast food or junk food. We may decline a place that does not fit the guide; if we do, you get a full refund.
  • Your contentSend us photos you own the rights to. We write the text in our own voice and check the facts with you before it goes up.
  • IndependencePaying never changes ratings, other places or what we write. If your place is not in the guide yet, we check it first and add it for free when it fits.
  • CancellingCancel a monthly subscription any time with the link in your Stripe email; it stops at the end of the month you paid for. Instagram posts and stories: full refund if you cancel before they go live.
